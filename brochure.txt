About NOUN Business School
Institutional Identity
NOUN Business School is a specialised graduate school of the National Open University of Nigeria, established to deliver
postgraduate management education — Graduate Diploma, MBA, MPA, DBA, DPA and Executive Certificate
Programmes — through a blended Open Distance Learning (ODL) model. Headquartered at the NOUN Headquarters,
University Village, Jabi, Abuja, the School is positioned at the seat of Nigeria's Federal Government, offering unrivalled
access to policymakers, regulators, diplomatic missions and a fast-growing technocratic and entrepreneurial class.

Our Commitment to Flexible, High-Quality Higher Education
NOUN Business School exists on the conviction that access and excellence are not competing goals. Our Open Distance
Learning model — combining weekend and evening study, intensive residencies, and digital learning platforms — is
built for the realities of Nigeria's working professionals, public servants, entrepreneurs and mid-career executives:
people for whom a conventional full-time campus programme is not a realistic option, but for whom rigorous,
internationally benchmarked business education is a professional necessity.
We hold ourselves to the same standards of scholarship, faculty quality and assessment rigour as any full-time
programme. What differs is the mode of delivery, not the depth of learning. Every track is designed around a practiceready curriculum, applied capstone projects, live consultancy engagements, and an Industry Partnership Consortium
that keeps our curriculum current and our graduates employable from the day they resume.

Vision
To be a leading centre of flexible, technology-enabled business and management education in Africa, producing globally
competitive and nationally committed leaders.

Mission
To deliver accessible, rigorous and practice-relevant graduate business education through Open Distance Learning,
equipping professionals across Nigeria and Africa with the strategic, technical and ethical competencies required to
lead organisations in a rapidly changing global economy.

Why Choose NOUN Business School?
• Flexible Global Open Distance Learning model suited to working professionals, public servants and entrepreneurs
• Pathway toward international accreditation and pan-African academic partnerships
• Practice-ready curriculum with a 60:40 ratio of domain expertise to applied, technology-enabled learning
• Capstone projects, live consultancy engagements and an Industry Partnership Consortium of leading
organisations
• Strategic Abuja location — proximity to the Federal Government, CBN, SEC, over 70 diplomatic missions and
multilateral agencies

1. Graduate Diploma in Business Administration (GDBA)
Programme Overview
The Graduate Diploma in Business Administration (GDBA) is the foundation pathway into the MBA/MPA programme,
designed for graduates of any discipline — as well as HND holders — who require a structured grounding in core
business and management fundamentals before proceeding to specialised postgraduate study. Delivered over one year
(two semesters) through a blended/Open Distance Learning model with weekend and evening study options, the GDBA
equips candidates with the conceptual toolkit and academic writing competence required for rigorous graduate-level
business education.
Award

Graduate Diploma in Business Administration (GDBA)

Duration

2 Semesters (minimum) / 4 Semesters (maximum)

Mode of Study

Open and Distance Learning (ODL) — blended print, e-learning, tutorials, and
examinations

Awarding Institution

National Open University of Nigeria, NOUN Business School

Philosophy & Objectives
• To provide a rigorous conversion pathway into postgraduate business study for non-business and HND graduates
• To build foundational competence in management, economics, accounting, marketing and quantitative methods
• To develop academic and professional communication skills required for graduate study
• To prepare candidates to proceed directly into the MBA programme upon satisfactory completion

Career Outcomes
• Direct progression into any of the 30 MBA/MPA specialisation tracks
• Enhanced eligibility for management-track roles in candidates' current organisations
• Strengthened analytical, communication and quantitative competence for early/mid-career professionals

Admission & Entry Requirements
First Degree (any discipline) from a recognised university with a minimum of Third Class, or HND with a minimum of
Lower Credit, plus five (5) O'Level credits including English Language and Mathematics. Candidates who complete the
GD with a satisfactory CGPA proceed directly to the MBA/MPA.

2. Master of Business Administration (MBA/MPA) — Thirty
Specialisation Tracks
Programme Overview
The MBA/MPA programme is a two-year (four-semester), Senate-approved graduate management qualification
comprising a Foundational Core taken by all candidates in Semester 1, followed by a chosen specialisation track from
Semester 2 onward, and concluding with a Capstone Project. The programme is delivered through a blended/Open
Distance Learning model with intensive weekend residencies, combining rigorous academic instruction with applied,
practice-based learning.
Award

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

Duration

4 Semesters (minimum) / 8 Semesters (maximum)

Mode of Study

Open and Distance Learning (ODL) — blended print, e-learning, tutorials, and
examinations

Awarding Institution

National Open University of Nigeria, NOUN Business School

Philosophy & Objectives
• To develop broad-based managerial competence alongside deep specialisation expertise
• To combine rigorous academic grounding with live, applied consultancy-style learning
• To produce graduates immediately effective in Nigerian and African organisational contexts
• To build networks of practice through cohort-based residencies and Industry Partnership engagement

General Admission & Entry Requirements (All MBA/MPA Tracks)
Bachelor's degree (minimum Second Class Lower) from a recognised university in any discipline, or HND (minimum
Upper Credit), plus five (5) O'Level credits including English Language and Mathematics. Holders of a Third Class/Lower
Credit may be considered with the GDBA or relevant cognate professional experience. A minimum of two (2) years postqualification work experience is required.

3. Doctor of Business Administration (DBA/DPA) — Thirty
Specialisation Tracks
Programme Overview
The DBA/DPA is a practitioner-research doctoral degree spanning three years: two years of coursework and doctoral
seminars, followed by a mandatory Field Global Immersion / International Study Tour, a Comprehensive Examination,
and a one-year period of Applied Doctoral Research culminating in dissertation defence. The programme is delivered
through blended Open Distance Learning with structured residencies and one-on-one supervision, designed for senior
practitioners seeking to generate original, practice-relevant management knowledge.
Award

Doctor of Business Administration (DBA/DPA)

Duration

6 Semesters (minimum) / 10 Semesters (maximum)

Mode of Study

Open and Distance Learning (ODL) — blended print, e-learning, tutorials, and
examinations

Awarding Institution

National Open University of Nigeria, NOUN Business School

Philosophy & Objectives
• To develop senior practitioners capable of generating original, rigorous, practice-relevant management research
• To bridge the gap between academic management theory and applied organisational problem-solving
• To expose candidates to global best practice through mandatory international immersion
• To produce thought leaders capable of shaping policy and practice within their specialisation

General Admission & Entry Requirements (All DBA/DPA Tracks)
A Master's degree (MBA or relevant Master's, minimum CGPA of 3.5 on a 5-point scale or equivalent) from a recognised
university, plus a minimum of five (5) years senior-level management or professional experience. Candidates must
submit a research concept note aligned to one of the specialisation tracks.

4. Executive Certificate Programmes (Non-Degree)
Programme Overview
The Executive Certificate Programmes are short, modular, non-degree qualifications designed for working professionals
seeking targeted upskilling. Delivered over 12–16 weeks in a weekend/evening modular format, each certificate is
credit-transferable toward the GDBA, MBA and MPA for candidates who later choose to pursue the full qualification.

Areas Offered
• Data Analytics
• FinTech
• ESG Reporting
• Project Management
• Public Procurement
• Entrepreneurship
• Healthcare Administration
• Digital Marketing

Entry Qualification
OND/HND/First Degree or relevant professional/work experience. Open to working professionals across all listed areas.

Graduation Requirements
• Satisfactory attendance and completion of all modular sessions
• Successful completion of the end-of-programme assessment/project
• Credits earned are transferable toward the GDBA or relevant MBA/MPA Foundational Core, subject to Academic
Board approval
